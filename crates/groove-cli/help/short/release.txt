EXAMPLES:
  groove release --save --task fix-parser
  groove release --reservation-id UUID

Stop ALL writers and preserve work first. Background preparation or the next acquisition
discards contents, including ignored files under pristine cleanup. Export ignored artifacts.
Preserve detached commits on a branch or snapshot before releasing.
--save pins a snapshot; save failure keeps the reservation.
--force skips unsaved-change and process checks; without --save, work can be lost.
