EXAMPLES:
  groove init --capacity 4
  groove init --capacity 4 --worktree-root /absolute/empty/root
  groove init --resume --capacity 4
  groove --pool frontend init --capacity 3 --cleanup warm

Creates slot metadata and permanent locks; checkouts are created lazily on first
acquisition. Running init on a ready pool fails with CONFIG_CONFLICT, even with
identical settings: use `config show` or `config set` instead. An interrupted
init must be resumed with --resume and the original settings.

Initializes the selected pool (--pool NAME; default: default). The checkout root
defaults to <GIT_COMMON_DIR>/groove/worktrees for default and
<GIT_COMMON_DIR>/groove/pools/<name>/worktrees for a named pool. It stays fixed for
that pool generation; after destroy, init can choose another root and settings.
A custom root must start empty, be owned by you and lie outside Git administration
and every checkout. Roots cannot overlap another pool's root, including pending
and retained deleted pools. Relative paths resolve from the current directory.
Never delete permanent lock files to force setup or recovery.
