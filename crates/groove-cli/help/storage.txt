EXAMPLES:
  groove storage show
  groove storage clean --logs --older-than 30d --dry-run
  groove storage clean --logs --older-than 30d
  groove storage clean --logs --older-than 0s

Cleanup requires --logs and defaults to runs completed/recovered at least 30 days
ago. It permanently deletes captured stdout/stderr, retaining history. --dry-run
previews the same checks. Sizes are logical bytes, not guaranteed reclaimed blocks.

Runs with unresolved outcomes and slots with active or orphaned executions are
skipped. Stop writers and explicitly recover an orphan before trying again. A
partial deletion is recorded and retryable; inspect each entry (exit 7 on failures).
Unknown/legacy log directories are reported but never automatically deleted.

This command does not delete workspaces, artifact caches, snapshots, reservation
records, saved-release receipts or CLI diagnostic logs. To forget history separately,
use `groove history delete RUN_ID` after cleaning that run's logs.
