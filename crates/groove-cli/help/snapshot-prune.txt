EXAMPLES:
  groove snapshot prune --older-than 30d
  groove snapshot prune --older-than 30d --keep-last 20 --apply
  groove snapshot prune --older-than 7d --keep-last 0 --dry-run

Preview is the default; --dry-run makes it explicit. --apply recomputes the policy
and deletes eligible snapshot references. A prior preview is informational, not
a frozen deletion plan. --older-than is required and accepts integer durations
in ms, s, m, h or d. 0s selects any snapshot older than the evaluation time.

Snapshots must be strictly older than the cutoff AND outside the newest
--keep-last snapshots per originating workspace (default 20). The workspace is
the saved Git directory, across all pools; a reused slot remains the same
workspace even when its task changes. Pinned snapshots count in the newest
window and are also retained independently. Future timestamps are retained.

Pins are always preserved; there is no --force override. Snapshots from durably
protected workspaces are conservatively retained to protect saved releases
between capture and pinning. Failed or interrupted in-place restores leave their
safety snapshots pinned; recover that work before explicitly deleting a pin with
snapshot delete --force. Corrupt protection or pin records fail closed.
Historical sources whose registrations are missing are also retained while any
durable reservation exists, because their ownership cannot be established safely.

The response names selected and deleted snapshots and gives a reason for each
retained snapshot. Selection and deletion hold the repository snapshot lock;
native Git transactions recheck snapshot identity and pin absence. A failure can
leave an already-deleted prefix; retry recomputes the remaining selection.

Deletion removes refs, not file objects immediately. Shared objects remain;
Git's ordinary garbage collection can reclaim unreferenced objects later.
Groove never runs GC, expires snapshots automatically, or deletes working files
as part of pruning. All linked worktrees share snapshot storage and retention.
