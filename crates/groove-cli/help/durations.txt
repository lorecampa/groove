DURATIONS AND SIZES
Durations use a whole integer followed by ms, s, m or h. Bare numbers are invalid.
--wait accepts zero (0s scans once) and limits waiting for capacity. Git operations
on a slot acquired by this caller use the separate positive --timeout budget.
Pool git-timeout defaults to 2m; a command override does not change the saved value.
idle-timeout and snapshot --older-than also accept d for days. idle-timeout accepts
never; snapshot --older-than accepts zero. No duration expires a reservation.

--max-file-size defaults to 100M. Bytes or K/M/G (powers of 1024) are accepted,
including KB/KiB suffixes, case-insensitively. Zero means unlimited.
